Question Generation & Flow

  • Resume Skill Extraction: When you upload a PDF resume, the parser scans the text layer for whole-word matches against an extensive technical skill library.
  • Role-Aligned System Prompt: Each session generates dynamic system instructions combining your target job role (e.g. Data Analyst, Software Engineer), detected skills, and interviewer persona.
  • 4-Stage Adaptive Flow:
    Stage 1: Greeting & Readiness Stage 2: Candidate Introduction Stage 3: Core Technical Competency Stage 4: Adaptive Deep-Dives
  • Full Session Memory: Every dialogue turn is recorded in SQLite. The complete turn history is sent to Gemini on each exchange with strict user/model alternation so the AI never loses conversational context.
📈

Scoring Rubrics & Report Generation

  • Technical Score (0-100): Evaluates factual correctness, depth, relevance to the job role, and problem-solving reasoning in candidate responses.
  • Communication Score (0-100): Evaluates structural clarity, articulation, conciseness, and professional tone in your explanations.
  • Overall Weighted Score: Computed using an industry-standard formula:
    Overall = (0.60 × Technical Score) + (0.40 × Communication Score)
  • Confidence Level (High / Moderate / Low): Evaluated based on verbal assertiveness, specificity, and completeness versus vague or hesitant phrasing.
  • Question Breakdown & Weak Topics: Every question is graded individually (0-100) with topic tags, benchmark model answers, and coaching critiques. Sub-70% scores automatically populate your Weak Topics tracker.
🛡️

System Transparency & What the AI Does NOT Do

Explicitly designed for fair, objective, text-based competency evaluation.

🚫
No Computer Vision / Video Tracking: The camera tile provides a local video mirror for candidate comfort. The system does not track eye contact, facial expressions, micro-emotions, or body posture.
🚫
No Voice Acoustics / Tone Analysis: Spoken answers are converted to text in real-time via the browser Web Speech API. Scoring evaluates the content, vocabulary, structure, and clarity of transcribed words, not pitch or volume.
✅
Objective Transcript Evaluation: All feedback, scores, and improvement suggestions derive strictly from the verbatim conversational transcript and standard hiring rubrics.
